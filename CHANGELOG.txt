SETTINGS UI MOD 2.0 — CHANGELOG
Changes since Settings UI Mod 1.7. Made for game version 2026-10-04.

UPGRADING FROM 1.7
  Put wayward-bridge.js and Start-Wayward-Bridge.bat in the game folder and start the
  .bat. Your old config.json (prompts, models, LoRAs, sizes, GIF and saved-picture
  settings), its presets.json and your add-on packs are brought over automatically the
  first time; the old files are not changed. Presets the old menu kept in your browser
  come over with Config > Import old presets. Saved scene pictures stay where they are.


ADDED

Your own heroines
  * "+ Build your own heroine" in the game's heroine picker (New game). It opens the
    builder the game already contains (name, age, background, looks, tendencies,
    portrait); your heroines are listed in the picker with Edit and Delete.
  * Works with every "Who is she to you?" choice, not only wife.
  * Your heroines get their own settings, saved-picture folders and add-ons, like the
    built-in heroines.

On the game's picture
  * New buttons: Show prompt (how the picture's prompt was built) and Edit (opens its
    prompts in the menu).
  * Saved < > arrows at the bottom right step through the scene's saved pictures one
    after another, with a counter such as "Saved 3/9". They wrap around at the ends
    and work whether Use Saved Images/GIFs is on or off.
  * The + button cycles through the scene's prompt variants (1, 2, 3…).
  * A thin progress bar with "step 12 / 24" while a picture is being drawn.
  * Short messages ("drawing…", "saved") pop up briefly instead of staying on screen.
  * Clicking the picture shows the prompt the mod really used, part by part.
  * The same buttons on the full-screen picture from the camera icon in the log; if a
    moment has no picture yet, Generate new draws one.
  * Keyboard shortcuts: Alt+G Generate new, Alt+S Same seed, Alt+Left / Alt+Right
    saved pictures, Alt+K save, Alt+X delete, Alt+P Show prompt, Alt+E Edit.

Prompt inspector (new tab)
  * Every picture drawn this session, with its prompt broken down part by part and
    who changed each part (your settings, an add-on, or the game).
  * The negative prompt is split by where each word comes from.
  * An Edit button on every row changes those words right where they come from.
  * "Preview with current settings" shows what a picture's prompt would be now,
    unsaved changes included.
  * "Use this scene" puts any earlier picture back in the game for its scene.
  * "Compare with…" shows two pictures side by side with the tags that differ.
  * "Compare models…" draws the same picture with the same seed but another model or
    LoRA strength, without changing your settings.
  * The drawing queue: what ComfyUI is drawing and what is waiting, with Cancel.

Negatives & tag rules (new tab)
  * The game's own negative prompt is editable: the list used for every picture and
    every extra it adds in some situations (patron scenes, clothing and exposure,
    rooms and the bath, her mood).
  * "Same game negatives for all heroines" makes one shared set.
  * Tag rules: find tags anywhere in the finished prompt and replace or remove them,
    or always add tags. A rule can be for all heroines or one, and limited to one part
    of the prompt (Mood, Pose…). "(2girls:1.3)", "2girls" and "2_girls" count as the
    same tag.
  * Suggestions from your ratings: after you have saved and deleted a few pictures,
    tags that keep showing up in the deleted ones are listed, each with a button that
    makes a tag rule. Nothing changes unless you press it.

Pipeline & models
  * Recommended size for your screen: measures the game's picture box in your window
    and suggests a size that fits it. "Fit picture to my screen" fills it in;
    "Restore defaults" puts it back.
  * Size advice follows your model type: SDXL models (Illustrious, Pony, NoobAI) and
    Anima / Z-Image models are each given sizes they draw well. The inspector warns
    when a picture was drawn at a size that tends to stretch or double bodies.
  * Picture size for single scenes: give one scene its own size.
  * Hi-res fix: draw, scale up, then add detail. Sharper, about twice as slow.
  * Face & hand fix: faces and hands are found and redrawn in more detail (needs the
    ComfyUI Impact Pack and Impact Subpack). Off by default.
  * Test pipeline: draws one small test picture and says in plain words what is wrong
    if it fails (ComfyUI not running, a missing model or custom node, out of graphics
    memory…).
  * LoRAs for chosen scenes or outfits only: their trigger words are added to just
    those pictures.

Patrons
  * Every man keeps the same face each time he is drawn. "Try a new face" draws him
    with another one; "Keep this face" makes it his.

Saved pictures
  * Browse saved pictures (Config): pick any heroine and scene, then star, move to
    another outfit / room / mood, or delete. Starred pictures are shown four times as
    often.
  * Generate all scenes: "Only fill gaps" tops each combination up to the count
    instead of adding that many more.

Menu
  * Undo and redo for your edits, step by step back to your last save (Ctrl+Z /
    Ctrl+Y). Config > Undo last save takes back a whole save.
  * Search prompts: type a few letters at the top of the menu to find every prompt
    that contains them.
  * The green bar under the tabs shows what the game is showing right now (scene,
    outfit, exposure, mood, room); click one to jump to its prompt.
  * Ctrl+Up / Ctrl+Down on a tag raises or lowers its weight: blush → (blush:1.1).
  * Long dropdowns (samplers, LoRAs…) can be searched.
  * Optional: Backspace / Delete remove whole tags (Config > Prompt boxes).
  * Renaming a heroine renames her in the game too, not only in the menu.

Add-ons
  * Use several add-ons at once.
  * Every prompt has a "Prompt from" dropdown: your settings or any add-on, a
    different one per prompt if you like. It can also save that one prompt into an
    add-on.
  * "Create add-on" makes one from just the parts you tick, including game negatives
    and tag rules.
  * Every add-on file is previewed before it is imported: what it contains and how
    many of your prompts it would change.

Config
  * Game update check: after a game update, lists the prompts the game changed, added
    or removed, and which of your edits were made for the old text.
  * Export everything / Import a backup: all settings, presets and add-ons in one
    file, to move to another PC. You see what is in the backup before it is used, and
    your folders and ComfyUI address can be kept.
  * Draw ahead while idle (off by default): recently shown scenes are drawn again in
    the background, so a fresh picture appears at once next time.
  * Diagnostics: whether the game, its prompt tables and ComfyUI were found.


CHANGED

  * Rebuilt from the ground up as one file, wayward-bridge.js, with
    Start-Wayward-Bridge.bat. Settings are kept in the wayward-bridge-data folder next
    to it.
  * The mod reads the game's own index.html each time, so it keeps working when the
    game updates. Replace the game's files and reload the page; no restart, no new mod
    needed unless the game changes how it builds its prompts.
  * New game default prompts come through after a game update: only the prompts you
    changed are stored.
  * Edits are drafts (orange border) until you press "Save & Apply to game". After
    saving, the picture on screen is drawn again with the new settings (can be turned
    off). Closing the menu with unsaved changes asks first.
  * Every playthrough keeps its own settings, even two games that started as the same
    heroine. A new game starts with that heroine's most recently played save's settings.
  * Saved for the heroine selected at the top: her prompts, model family, models,
    sampler, which LoRAs are used, turbo and patron portraits. LoRA trigger words and
    groups, picture sizes, quality tags, the hi-res fix, face fix, GIF settings and
    Config are shared by all heroines.
  * Tabs: "Server & defaults" is now "Config"; new "Negatives & tag rules" and
    "Prompt inspector" tabs.
  * A tidier menu: each tab has its own colour, and its sections, buttons and Save
    button use it. Sections share one look, buttons come in three kinds (normal,
    main, delete), and the header fits on one line.
  * The "Gen All" button is gone: every picture is drawn new until you turn on Use
    Saved Images/GIFs. With it on, Generate new and Same seed still draw a new picture.
  * Thumbs up saves the current scene; thumbs down deletes it and draws a new one
    (asks first). On a saved picture, thumbs down deletes that saved file.
  * The game's video button turns the picture into a looping GIF.
  * Buttons on the game's picture, the mod's and the game's own, only show while the
    mouse is over it (a tap shows them on touch screens), so the picture stays clear.
  * Saved pictures are kept per heroine. Saving works without a ComfyUI output
    folder set (they then go in the wayward-bridge-data folder).
  * Danbooru tag suggestions while typing: about 81,000 tags, other spellings (aliases)
    lead to the right tag, and choosing works like an IME list (Up / Down, Enter or
    Tab, Esc). Only the letters you typed are replaced.
  * The default scene size is 1216 × 832, the shape of the game's picture box.
  * Old add-on packs can still be imported (Config > Add-ons), and old presets
    (Config > Import old presets).


BUGFIXES

  * Works with the current game version. 1.7 looked for parts of the game by names
    that change with every game build, so some features stopped working after game
    updates; 2.0 finds them by what they contain.
  * "Restore defaults" works again (it failed with an error).
  * "Unload add-on" works again (it did nothing).
  * TeaCache / SDXL cache settings no longer turn themselves off when ComfyUI is
    offline or the cache node is not found.
  * A missing cache node in ComfyUI is skipped instead of making ComfyUI refuse the
    whole picture.
  * Switching heroine or loading an add-on no longer empties the character prompt.
  * Removed LoRA trigger words no longer come back, or get lost, when the menu is used
    in another browser.
  * Thumbs up saves the picture you are looking at, not the last picture drawn, and
    pressing it twice no longer saves a second copy.


MISC / OTHER

  * Other websites you visit can no longer change your settings, start drawings on
    your graphics card or read your pictures; only the game page on your own computer
    can.
  * ComfyUI is only contacted over its web address; it is never started or stopped.
  * Every picture drawn is kept in a library with its prompt and seed, and the last 50
    versions of your settings are kept as backups.
  * Options for the .bat: --port, --game (game folder), --host 0.0.0.0 (play from a
    phone on your network).
  * Your own ComfyUI workflow can replace the built-in one (see README).
  * Needs Node.js 18 or newer.
